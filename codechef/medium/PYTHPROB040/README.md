### Calculate Total Cost

- Each cookie costs $1.
- You buy 3 cupcakes and 5 cookies.

 **Expected Output** 
After performing the calculations, your program should print the total cost as follows:

```
11

```

Feel free to modify the quantities and prices to see how the total cost changes!
