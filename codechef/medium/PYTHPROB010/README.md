## Problem

### The Game of Number Sum

You are running a game where kids pick balls labeled with both positive and negative numbers. Your goal is to calculate the total sum of the numbers on three selected balls.

 **Ball Values** 
• Ball 1: 20
• Ball 2: -3
• Ball 3: 5

 **Expected Output** 
When you add 20, -3, and 5, the sum should be:

```
22

```

After you write your code, run it to verify the total is correct. Then proceed to the next step!
