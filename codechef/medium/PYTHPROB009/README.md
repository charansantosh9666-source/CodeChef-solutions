## Problem

### Total Cost of Fast Food Order

You’ve ordered a burger, fries, and a drink at a fast food restaurant. Your goal is to sum their individual prices using Python’s '+' operator.

 **Item Prices** 
• Burger: $5
• Fries: $2
• Drink: $1

 **Expected Output** 

```
Total cost of the order is: 8

```

Once you’ve coded your solution, confirm that the total is correct. Then you can move on to the next step

## Solution

**Language:** Python  
**Runtime:** N/A  
**Memory:** N/A  
**Submitted:** 2026-09-01T11:36:27.092Z  

```py
# Prices of the items
burger_price = 5
fries_price = 2
drink_price = 1
# Calculate total cost here
total_cost = burger_price+fries_price+drink_price

# Print the total cost
print("Total cost of the order is:", total_cost)
```
